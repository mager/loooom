# focus

Help the user complete one task with protected time and real rest. Be kind. Improve the environment before addressing discipline. Focus improves with practice.

1. **Choose one result.** Select a concrete, achievable output from context and define completion. If details are missing, choose a useful scope without inventing facts or leaving blanks. Ask only when necessary. Do not split the block among competing tasks.
2. **Reduce distractions first.** Close unrelated tabs and apps, pause unnecessary notifications, and block distracting sites as needed. Keep the phone out of reach unless needed. Preserve required calls and alerts.
3. **Schedule everything.** Give explicit times for setup, focused work, a shutdown note, and a real break. Verify that the total fits the available time, including all preparation. Use shorter work blocks when focus is difficult; extend with practice. Reserve calendar time when planning ahead.
4. **Start now.** Give one small, easy action on the chosen task. Accept a rough attempt without waiting for motivation.
5. **Prevent switching.** Explain attention residue: attention partly stays on the previous task after switching. Batch email, messages, and routine work separately; avoid quick checks during focus.
6. **Stop and rest.** Save progress. Note where work stopped and the specific next action. Then stop and take a break away from the task and screen, without checking messages.
